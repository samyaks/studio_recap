Privacy

Your members' data stays in your browser

When you load an attendance export, it is read and processed on your own computer, in your browser. It is not uploaded to us or stored on any server we run. Each member's recap is packed into their own link, after the # sign. Browsers don't send that part of a link to any web server, so opening a recap doesn't send the member's data to us either. Anyone who has a member's link can see that member's recap, so send each link only to that member.

What we do receive

  • When you buy: Stripe handles payment. We receive your name, email address, the studio name and year you enter, and the payment status. We never see your card number. We use your email to send your unlock code and to answer you. The code email is sent through Resend, which receives your email address for that purpose.
  • When a page breaks: if a script error happens, the page sends us the page name, the error text and your browser type, so we can fix it. It never sends member data or the part of a link after the # sign. We keep these reports for 30 days.
  • When you email us: we keep the messages you send.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may log basic request details such as IP address and time to run and protect its network. Stripe, Resend and Cloudflare may process data in the United States. We don't use advertising trackers or sell any data.

Your responsibility for member data

You decide which members get a recap and you send the links yourself. You are responsible for having the right to use your members' attendance data this way and to email them, under your own privacy policy and the laws that apply to you.

Questions and deletion

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you (your purchase record and emails), write to us. We keep purchase records as long as tax law requires.
