How to send them
  1. Import the CSV into your email tool, or into your booking system's email feature. Each row has the member's email, first name, which message they get, and their personal link.
  2. Make two emails: one for recap rows (subject idea: "Your year at the studio is here") and one for welcome_back rows (subject idea: "Your spot is saved for January").
  3. Put the link column in as a merge field behind a button like "See my year".
  4. Send a test to yourself first and tap the link on your phone.

If your tool shortens or breaks long links, turn on Short links in step 3 and download again.
